Earth riser with floor earth bars, any number of floors (mockup)

Drawn as your reference sheet: a floor earth bar with two test links on every level, one 1C G/Y earth cable looping bar to bar up the riser, 150 mm² from the LV room earth bar to the ground-floor bar. DBs are not on this sheet; they go on a separate floor-level sheet (bottom). Pick the number of floors to see how it scales.
Floors above ground:

Option A: full riser, split over sheets (max 20 levels per sheet)

Every level drawn. A match line at the top of a sheet continues on the next sheet (E-ERT-01, -02 …). 100 floors = 6 sheets.

Option B: one sheet, typical floors grouped

Floors with the same earthing (same bar, same cable) are drawn once as "TYPICAL LEVEL-05 TO LEVEL-95"; the first, last and any floor that differs (plant, refuge, roof) are drawn in full. Always fits on one sheet.

Both options

  • Bar tag: EB-EL-A-L03 (EB + riser EL-A + level). A bar that also takes the DBs of that floor can be named EBN as on your sheet.
  • Riser cable size and LV room cable size are project settings (default 95 / 150 mm² 1C G/Y PVC).
  • The cable enters one test link and leaves from the other, alternating sides, so any bar can be isolated for testing.
  • More than one riser (EL-A, EL-B) is drawn side by side.
  • Black and white, A3 sheet, same frame as the other earthing sheets.

Separate sheet: floor DB earthing (one per floor, or one per typical floor)

The floor earth bar from the riser sheet, and each SMDB / DB earth bar on that floor connected to it with its CPC size. A DB fed from another DB on the same floor goes to that DB's bar.
