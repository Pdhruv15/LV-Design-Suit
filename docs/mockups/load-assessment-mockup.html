Supply settings
How it works: area × W/m² per space type gives lighting, small power and cooling. Cooling is converted to electrical kW by the chosen cooling system (kW per kW-thermal). Services add their own kW. Each category gets its own diversity (edit in the table); fire pumps and life-safety fans are on the generator only and stay out of normal MD. The sum is multiplied by the coincidence factor and future spare, then checked against the type's VA/m² band.
