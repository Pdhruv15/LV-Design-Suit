  • Open natively: the plan fills a CAD model space (dark or light), with a crosshair cursor, live coordinates, snap markers (endpoint, midpoint, intersection, perpendicular, centre), pan with the middle button / space-drag, wheel zoom at the cursor, Fit, and the architect's layers (on / off). Blocks are drawn as drawn (doors, furniture, symbols).
  • Measure length (DI): two snapped points → length, ΔX, ΔY, shown on the plan and in Result; ORTHO for straight runs.
  • Check area (AREA): click the corners (snapped) or pick a closed outline → area and perimeter; Room area clicks inside a room (walls followed, doors closed). Results can be sent to Building information as a room.
  • Add device (DEV): pick a symbol (light, socket, DB, ELV devices…) and its circuit, click on the plan (snapped), it is placed on its layer and counted in the design; Move / Delete as in CAD.
  • Command line for engineers used to CAD (DI, AREA, DEV, ESC to cancel); every command also on the ribbon. Save DXF keeps the architect's drawing plus the devices added.
  • Built on the Rust engine (WebAssembly) in the desktop app and the web version. Not now: drawing / editing the architect's walls, dimensions, plotting and submission sheets — added one by one when asked.
